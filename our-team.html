Meet Our Dedicated Team

Our clinicians combine extensive experience, empathetic understanding, and evidence-based approaches to guide your mental wellness journey.

Hilda Mary Nabukonde

Advanced Diploma in Bible Study (Australia) | B.A. in Counselling Psychology (Tangaza University) | Master’s Degree (Ongoing)

Hilda Mary Nabukonde is a dedicated counselling professional specializing in trauma therapy. She brings a unique integration of faith-based and evidence-based psychological care to her practice.


Currently pursuing her Master's degree, Hilda focuses on supporting adolescents and young adults navigating difficult experiences, providing safe spaces for trauma recovery and holistic healing.

Dennis Murabula Obuya, MSc, BSc

Oncology Specialist | Clinical Psychologist | Onco-Psychology Expert

Dennis Murabula Obuya is a seasoned oncology professional with over 20 years of experience in cancer care. He holds a Bachelor's in Radiotherapy and a Master's in Clinical Psychology.


Specializing in onco-psychology, Dennis addresses the emotional and social impacts of cancer on patients and caregivers, fostering emotional resilience throughout treatment and survivorship.
